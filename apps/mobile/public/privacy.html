Privacy

Last updated 2 September 2026

18-0 is a football game. It does not want your identity, and it is built so that it does not need one.

There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers. This page describes everything the game actually stores.

Playing without an account

You can play without signing in or giving us anything. Casual games are held on your device and never sent anywhere.

What is stored when you play ranked

Ranked play puts a score on a shared leaderboard, so it needs something to attach that score to. The game creates an anonymous account automatically: a random identifier, with no email address, no password, and no name attached to it unless you choose one.

Stored on the server:

  • An account identifier. A random value. It is not derived from your device, your phone number, or anything about you.
  • A display name, if you set one. You choose it, it is visible to everyone on the leaderboard, and it can be changed once a month. Until you set one you are given a generated placeholder.
  • Your ranked games. The roster you built, the rating and record it earned, and when it was played.
  • Reports you file about another player's display name, so the same name is not reported twice by the same person.
  • A record of server requests. Each request the game makes to the server is logged with a request identifier, the account that made it, what was asked for, whether it was allowed or refused, how long it took, and which kind of client sent it. This is how abuse and forged scores are detected, and how a disputed leaderboard entry can be checked. It contains no message content and nothing about you beyond the account identifier.

What is never collected

  • Email addresses and passwords, unless you sign in with one deliberately.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data.
  • Precise or coarse location.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, AAID) — no ad network is present.
  • Behaviour on other apps or websites.

Gameplay statistics stay on your device. The game keeps a local record of how a session went — spins taken, time spent, which positions stall people — so the game can be tuned. It is held in memory on the device and is not transmitted anywhere. There is no analytics service connected to this app.

Where it is kept

On Supabase, in a database hosted in Northern California, United States. If you are outside the United States, playing ranked means that data is stored there.

The web version is served by GitHub Pages, which receives the ordinary request information any web server does, including your IP address. That is GitHub's handling, described in their privacy statement.

Deleting it

Delete my account is in the game, on the leaderboard screen. It does not require contacting anyone. It permanently removes your account, your display name, and every ranked game attached to it. Your leaderboard entries disappear with them.

One thing survives, and it is worth being plain about. The record of server requests described above is append-only — nothing, including the operators of this game, can edit or delete a row once it is written. That is deliberate: a log that could be quietly rewritten could not be used to settle a dispute about a score.

What remains in it is the random account identifier and what that account asked the server to do. After deletion that identifier no longer corresponds to any account, any name, or any game. It is retained because an integrity record that can be erased by the person it records is not an integrity record.

Children

18-0 is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them. There is no way for one player to send another a message, and the only thing a player can publish is a display name, which is filtered on entry and can be reported by anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete what is held about you, and to object to its processing. Deletion is built into the game and is immediate. For anything else, use the contact address below.

Because accounts are anonymous by design, we usually cannot connect a request to an account without the account identifier — which you can find in the game. Asking for it is not an attempt to collect more about you; it is the only link that exists.
