ON THE 4.3 / 4.2.6 REVIEW OF NOV 5. Our Resolution Center reply that day answers Apple's nine questions in full. In short:
- Original game, not a variant of a category. 17-0 is a football history puzzle: no league, no weekly matchups, no live scoring, no drafting against other people, no trivia question bank, no money. A spin deals a franchise and an era; you take one real season from it, seven times, and an era-adjusted rating model of our own projects a 17-game record.
- Original content. 4,872 real player seasons across 32 franchises and 7 eras, rated by a deterministic model the server re-runs to verify every ranked season.
- Not built from a template or an app generation service, and not made for anyone else's content. I designed it and am the content provider; the code was written for this game, largely with an AI coding assistant under my direction.
- Since that review: the store name now says what the game is ("17-0: Perfect Season Builder"), a new icon and design, a two-page layout for iPhone Duo, and an in-app What's New.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE. For teenage and adult football fans. The goal is a perfect 17-0 season, about once in 6,000 games. No wagers, prizes, purchases or ads.

NO DEMO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED. The app signs in anonymously on first launch, with no prompt and no sign-up screen. The app opens on the Play tab: scroll to the three modes and tap one (Play Rookie shows every rating, Play Scout shows stat lines only, Play GM Mode shows names only), then spin and pick seven times; Reveal your result shows the rating and record. The Ranks tab, second in the bottom bar, holds the leaderboards.

SIGNING IN IS OPTIONAL. The Account screen (avatar disc, top right of any screen) offers Sign in with Apple and Google; nothing in the game is gated behind either. Signing in keeps your seasons and puts a ranked season on the public board.

4.8: Sign in with Apple is offered wherever Google is, enforced in code: if Google is configured without Apple, iOS shows no sign-in buttons at all.

5.1.1(v): Account deletion is in the app. Account screen -> "Delete my account", just below the sign-in buttons -> "Permanently delete my account". No sign-in, email or support contact needed. It removes the account, display name, every ranked season and the analytics for that account.

1.2: The only user-generated content is a display name on the leaderboard. Names are checked against a denylist by the database when set, so a blocked name is refused rather than published. Every name on every leaderboard carries a report flag (the small flag at the top right of each podium card and at the end of each row), and a reported name can be hidden.

EXTERNAL SERVICES. Supabase (database, anonymous and optional sign-in, server-side scoring of ranked seasons, leaderboard). PostHog (first-party analytics and feature flags; no advertising identifier, no tracking). Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In (optional). Season statistics from nflverse (CC BY 4.0) and Pro-Football-Reference (licensed), bundled at build time. No AI services.

REGIONS. No differences. The app is the same in every territory and plays fully offline; only the leaderboard needs a connection.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL. No team names, logos, marks or colours: franchises are identified by city only and every palette is generated. Player names appear with statistics from their real seasons, factual data used as fantasy football and sports-reference sites use it. No player photographs or likenesses are shown.
