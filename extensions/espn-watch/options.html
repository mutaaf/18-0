Sponsorship

Put {sponsor} anywhere in the copy below and it is filled in. Leave the name empty and the token — and the words holding it — disappear, so the copy reads correctly either way.

Whether the line is drawn at all is the bottom-right slot below. It used to be a switch of its own here, and two switches for one question is one of them somebody turns off looking for the other.

Slots

The four corners of the artwork, in the tile and in the band. A corner is a place and does not move; what stands in it, and whether anything does, is set here. An image is either a file this extension ships — icons/crest.png — or an https:// address. Anything else is ignored.

Tile copy

The card that sits in a row. The tagline sits under the mark; the headline is dropped at this size — three words beside a 300px card's wordmark either wrap into the button or shrink past reading — so it is here for the band to use.

Header copy

The band that sits between rows.

Call to action

A link out, beside the copy on the tile and the band and in the panel's footer. Never the main action — that is playing — and it opens in a new tab. {sponsor} works in both fields.

Attribution

Counted on this machine and written to local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere — there is no endpoint to send it to.

Your ESPN account

Linking lets a season carry your ESPN identity so it can rank on a shared leaderboard. Your identifier is hashed with a random salt on this device; the value itself is never stored or sent, and the hash cannot be turned back into it. Unlinking deletes both, so linking again produces a different identity with nothing joining the two.
