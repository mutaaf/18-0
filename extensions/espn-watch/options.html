Call to action

A link out, beside the copy on the tile and the band and in the panel's footer. Never the main action — that is playing — and it opens in a new tab. {sponsor} works in both fields.

Logo

Shown on the tile and on the band. Leave the URL empty for the 18-0 crest that ships with the extension; point it anywhere else and that image is used instead.

Attribution

Counted on this machine and written to local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere — there is no endpoint to send it to.

Your ESPN account

Linking lets a season carry your ESPN identity so it can rank on a shared leaderboard. Your identifier is hashed with a random salt on this device; the value itself is never stored or sent, and the hash cannot be turned back into it. Unlinking deletes both, so linking again produces a different identity with nothing joining the two.
